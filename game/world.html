FLY

  • Drag the grip of the joystick, or use ← → ↑ ↓ to turn and pitch. Q E roll.
  • W pushes the throttle, S brakes. Hold the red button or Shift to boost.
  • Hold the trigger (index finger) or Space to fire. Tab or the thumb switch pins a hazard. B or the commander reopens the briefing. N labels the sky.

THE SKY IS REAL

Everything outside is placed for 10 February 2009, 17:00 UT, about when Iridium 33 hit Cosmos 2251. The Earth has its real continents and city lights, the Sun and Moon (almost full) sit where they were, and so do Mercury to Neptune, with their real brightness, phase and pole tilt. Stars come from a real catalogue. Aim the reticle at a planet and the target display shows it close up.

WORLD STYLE

Retro draws the whole 3D world on the cockpit's own 480 by 300 pixel grid with a dithered palette. Hi-res and Smooth keep the same cockpit but render the world at full resolution. Debris is drawn far bigger than life so you can see it.

Prototype. Debris names, sizes and altitudes come from the real data pool; the model's estimate is a stand-in. Sources: star positions from d3-celestial (BSD-3), coastlines and cities from Natural Earth (public domain), positions from astropy.
